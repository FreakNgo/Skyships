HOW TO PLAY

W / ↑ · S / ↓Climb · dive (I inverts) · on the ground S brakes A / ← · D / →Bank & turn (steers on the ground) · Q / E rudder Shift · CtrlThrottle up · down · Z full · X idle · mouse wheel BWheel brake SpaceAfterburner boost F · P · M · RFullscreen (forced by default — change in Settings) · pause · mute · restart GamepadLeft stick fly · RT / LT throttle · A boost · B brake TouchDrag left side to steer · slider = throttle · BOOST button
  • Take-off: you start on the runway. Hold Shift (or slide the throttle up) to roll, and at about 200 km/h pull back with W / ↑. The wheels fold away by themselves.
  • Landing: line up with the runway, keep the wings level and touch down gently. Wheels come down automatically near the ground.
  • Taxi: after landing keep a low throttle, steer with A / D, brake with S, then follow the curved taxiway round the end of the runway and back to line up for another take-off. Grass slows you down.
  • Ceiling: stay under 1,600 m. Higher up the air is too thin and the jet is pushed down.
  • Crash: mountains, lakes, trees, houses and city buildings are solid — hit one and the jet explodes.
  • Score: distance flown, plus bonus points for flying low (22–90 m above ground). Look for cities and cosy villages!
  • Warnings: “PULL UP” means terrain is dead ahead. “STALL” means you are too slow — lower the nose.
